## 1. The Mandatory 3-Step Pathology Reasoning Chain

Every clinical interpretation generated by the AI must strictly follow this 3-step chain:

$$\text{Quantitative Feature (Metric)} \longrightarrow \text{Biological Meaning (Tissue Biology)} \longrightarrow \text{Qualified Statement (Interpretation)}$$

> [!CAUTION]

## 2. Controlled Pathology Vocabulary (Preferred Terms)

| Avoid Casual / Non-Medical Language | Mandatory Controlled Pathology Term | Clinical & Biological Significance |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| *"cells look weird"* / *"bad cells"* | **Nuclear pleomorphism / atypia** | Variation in nuclear size, shape, and chromatin density. |
| *"glands messed up"* | **Architectural distortion / glandular disorganization** | Irregular, branching, fused, or back-to-back cribriform crypts. |
| *"dark nuclei"* / *"black spots"* | **Hyperchromasia** | Increased nuclear DNA content staining deeply with Hematoxylin. |
| *"cells stacked up"* | **Nuclear stratification / pseudostratification** | Nuclei piling up at multiple vertical levels within the epithelium. |
| *"breaking through"* | **Invasion through muscularis mucosae into submucosa** | The definitive diagnostic criterion distinguishing adenocarcinoma from adenoma. |
| *"pre-cancer"* | **Dysplastic Adenoma (low-grade or high-grade)** | Neoplastic proliferation confined by an intact basement membrane. |
| *"dead tissue in gland"* | **Luminal dirty necrosis** | Eosinophilic necrotic cellular debris and apoptosis in glandular lumens. |
| *"messy borders"* | **Loss of nuclear and cellular polarity** | Disorientation of nuclear long axes away from the basal membrane. |
| *"cells dividing fast"* | **Increased mitotic activity / atypical mitoses** | Mitotic figures extending beyond the basal crypt zone into the upper mucosa. |

## 3. Tri-Cohort Histological Comparison (Ground-Truth Criteria)

| Morphological Feature | Normal Colonic Mucosa (`NORM`) | Dysplastic Adenoma (`ADENOMA`) | Invasive Adenocarcinoma (`TUM`) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Basement Membrane** | Strictly intact | Strictly intact | **Disrupted / penetrated** |
| **Invasion Depth** | No invasion | Confined to mucosa | **Invasion through muscularis mucosae into submucosa** |
| **Gland Architecture** | Straight, test-tube like, parallel test tubes with ample intervening stroma | Crowded, branched, tubular or villous architecture with diminished stroma | Haphazardly infiltrative, cribriform, single-file or poorly formed irregular lumens |
| **Desmoplasia** | Absent | Absent | **Prominent desmoplastic fibrous stromal reaction** |
| **Nuclear Polarity** | Basally oriented, uniform, oval | Pseudostratified, enlarged, elongated pencil-shaped nuclei | Complete loss of polarity, round to pleomorphic, haphazard orientation |
| **Chromatin** | Finely dispersed, uniform | Mild to moderate hyperchromasia | Coarse, vesicular with prominent, eosinophilic nucleoli |
| **Luminal Dirty Necrosis** | Absent | Absent (occasional apoptotic bodies) | **Frequently present (pathognomonic of aggressive CRC)** |
| **Mitotic Activity** | Confined strictly to lower 1/3 of crypts | Extends to upper 2/3 and surface epithelium | Frequent, abnormal, multipolar, throughout all tissue levels |

## 4. Features That Must NEVER Be Interpreted Independently

1. **Nuclear enlargement alone** is not diagnostic of malignancy (frequently occurs in reactive atypia, ulcerative colitis, or regeneration).
2. **Hyperchromasia alone** is not diagnostic (significantly influenced by section thickness and hematoxylin staining duration).
3. **High mitotic count alone** is not diagnostic (must be localized to crypt zone; basal proliferation is physiological).
4. **Gland crowding alone** is not diagnostic (can artificially occur due to **tangential / oblique sectioning** angles).
5. **No single computational metric is diagnostic.** Definitive diagnosis requires integrated architectural, cytological, and invasive-status assessment by a qualified pathologist.

## 5. WHO Adenocarcinoma Differentiation Grading

* **Well Differentiated (Grade 1):** $> 95\%$ of tumor composed of well-formed, recognizable glandular structures.
* **Moderately Differentiated (Grade 2):** $50\% - 95\%$ glandular structures with progressive architectural irregularity.
* **Poorly Differentiated (Grade 3):** $< 50\%$ glandular structures; predominant solid sheets, cords, or single infiltrative cells.

## 6. Molecular & Immunohistochemistry (IHC) Correlation

For borderline morphological cases or poorly differentiated tumors, standard H&E morphology must be supplemented by:
* **Mismatch Repair (MMR) Proteins:** MLH1, MSH2, MSH6, PMS2 staining to assess DNA mismatch repair deficiency (dMMR).
* **Microsatellite Instability (MSI):** PCR/NGS testing for MSI-High status to determine eligibility for PD-1/PD-L1 checkpoint immunotherapy.
* **Somatic Mutations:** KRAS, NRAS, and BRAF V600E mutational analysis.
